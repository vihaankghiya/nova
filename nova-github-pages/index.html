✦ NOVA AI OS Open prototype

A desktop OS with an AI assistant built in.

NOVA AI OS is a PC operating system concept. It has a desktop, apps, wallpapers and JARVIS, a voice assistant you can talk to.

Try the prototype See the roadmap

What it is

Desktop

Draggable windows, a dock, a launcher with search and a clock.

Apps

Files, Terminal, Settings and Browser, plus shortcuts to WhatsApp, YouTube, Gmail, Maps, Spotify, Instagram and Google.

JARVIS

A voice assistant that answers in chat and speaks its replies aloud.

Wallpapers

Upload your own image or use the animated built-in wallpapers.

What works today

Working windows

Open, move and close apps like a real desktop.

Live app shortcuts

Map, Spotify and YouTube open inside the desktop. Other apps open in a new tab.

Voice and chat

Ask JARVIS a question by typing or by speaking.

This is a browser prototype of the desktop, not a full operating system.
